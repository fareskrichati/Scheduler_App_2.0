# UniPlan 4.0

## Plan your next term before the current one ends

- Save dates, the last day of finals, and breaks for each semester, quarter, or trimester under Classes.
- Import your next schedule while your current term is still in progress. Meetings appear immediately on their scheduled calendar dates. Each future term has its own expandable **Upcoming** section in Classes.
- When a term begins, its courses move into the main Classes list. They stay there through the last day of finals when that date is set. Past meetings and finals stay on their original calendar dates.
- Schedule imports use the selected term’s breaks and skip meetings you’ve already imported.

## Find term dates and add events

- **Find calendar manually** opens a search for your school’s academic calendar. Use it to look up term dates, finals, and breaks, then enter them in UniPlan. The automatic research and autofill option was removed.
- **Add event** opens the event form. The event screenshot reader was removed. You can also add events through a connected AI app.

## Get started with the UniPlan connector

- In Settings, open **Connected AI apps**, then **Why connect? See what you can ask**. The guide includes example prompts for looking up your schedule, adding items, and importing a weekly class schedule. It also explains read and write permissions.
- Classes, Events, Homework, and Exams link to connector setup. For homework and exams, use Canvas import when available or share the details with your connected AI app.

## See weekdays in To-Do

To-Do dates now include the weekday, for example **Sunday, Nov 15**.
